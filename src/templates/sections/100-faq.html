Have Questions?

Find answers to the most common questions about our mission.

How can I volunteer?
It's simple! Click the "Contact Us" button in the navigation bar, fill out our volunteer application form, and our community manager will get back to you within 48 hours.
Where do the donations go?
100% of public donations go directly to funding our on-the-ground projects. We cover our operational costs through private grants and corporate sponsorships.
Can I start a local chapter?
Absolutely. We provide a starter kit and mentorship for passionate individuals effectively looking to bring GreenCare to their local community.
How do you measure impact?
We use satellite imagery, ground surveys, and third-party audits to track every project. Quarterly impact reports are published publicly on our website for full transparency.
Is my donation tax-deductible?
Yes. GreenCare is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. You'll receive a receipt via email immediately after donating.
